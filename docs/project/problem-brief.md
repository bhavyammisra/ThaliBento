# JAL-PS-01: Understanding & Fulfilling Diverse Dietary Needs of Indian Passengers
Japan Airlines · Track: In-Flight Hospitality & Dietary Intelligence · Verbatim from the hackathon site (hack.tslasconnect.com)

> How can airlines better understand and fulfill the diverse dietary needs of Indian passengers throughout their journey?
> The challenge extends beyond meal pre-booking to include changing or last-minute requirements.

## Background
India has a highly diverse food culture shaped by religion, culture, region, health, and personal preferences. For airlines serving Indian passengers, accommodating these varied needs can be challenging.

Airlines largely depend on passengers to communicate special meal requirements in advance. However, passengers may forget, change their preferences, or communicate their needs only at the airport or during the journey, making fulfillment difficult at short notice.

## What your solution should address
- Better understand and capture passenger dietary needs across cultures, religions, and regions
- Respond dynamically to changing or last-minute dietary requirements
- Coordinate effectively across passengers, airline ground staff, airport lounges, and catering teams
- Provide suitable hot meals or viable premium alternatives when required on short notice

## What to build
Develop a working prototype, product concept, service model, or technology-driven solution addressing one or more passenger touchpoints:
**Before Journey → Airport → Boarding → In-flight**

Some directions you could take:
- AI-driven personalization & proactive dietary profiling
- Smarter multi-channel passenger communication
- Predictive demand forecasting & intelligent buffer calculation
- Last-minute fulfillment & airport-to-cabin catering synchronization
- Cabin crew assistive tools & digital inventory management
